## Frozen / permitted change boundary

All weights, encoding/ROI, candidate/filter/ranking/route/maximum-participation masks, order, action/goal/KEEP/WAIT, cadence/ownership/cancellation, Nav2/SLAM/physical/safety profile above are part of this reference. Changing them needs a new version and affected comparison baselines, even if weights stay6d. Semantics-preserving transport serialization/scheduling/compression may be researched separately, with source/receipt identity and behavior intact. No ideal ROS/shared-memory bypass can fill undelivered observations or hide a different allocator. Hardware tuning that changes ROI, sensors or execution/safety semantics is not silently covered by this tag.
